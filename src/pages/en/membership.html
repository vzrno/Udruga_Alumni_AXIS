Who can join, membership categories and how to pay the annual fee

How to join

Membership is open to anyone who completed a professional undergraduate or graduate programme at the University Department of Professional Studies, and to current students. Membership is voluntary; by submitting the application form you accept the objectives, rights and obligations set out in the statute.

  1. Fill in the online application below — or download the PDF form, complete it and send it to alumniaxis.st@gmail.com or hand it in at the association's office.
  2. The Secretary enters you in the register of members and emails you.
  3. Active members pay the annual fee using the payment details.
The forms and official decisions are in Croatian. If you need help completing them, write to us in English and we will walk you through it.

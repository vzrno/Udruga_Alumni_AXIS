What we collect

This site sets no tracking cookies, shows no advertising and does not profile visitors. We only receive data that you send us yourself:

  • Contact form: your name, email address, topic and message. Used only to answer your enquiry.
  • Membership application: the details on the form, needed to maintain the register of members required by the Croatian Associations Act and our statute.

Legal basis and retention

Enquiries rest on your consent (Article 6(1)(a) GDPR) and are kept for at most two years. Membership data rests on performance of the membership relationship and our legal obligation to keep a register of members (Article 6(1)(b) and (c)); it is kept for the duration of membership and for the statutory periods after it ends.

Who receives the data

We never sell data or share it for marketing. Because of how the site works, these processors are involved:

  • the hosting provider — keeps access logs (IP address, time of request) for security and server operation;
  • the form-handling service — receives a message sent through the form and forwards it to the association's mailbox;
  • Google Maps — the map on the contact page loads only after you click to open it, because loading it passes your IP address to that service.

Fonts and stylesheets are served from our own domain, so simply visiting the site sends nothing to third parties.
